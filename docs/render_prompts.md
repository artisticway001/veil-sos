# VEIL - product render prompts

Paste the CONTEXT block first, then one product prompt at a time.

## CONTEXT (paste first)
I'm a product designer. Our hackathon product is VEIL, a women's safety SOS system that works without internet or phone signal (for example in underground parking basements). It has 4 parts: a wearable bracelet, a one-button keychain, a wall-mounted basement receiver, and a guard desk unit. I need photorealistic product-design renders (studio, Keyshot/Blender style) for a pitch deck. Style for every image: minimal, premium, soft-tech. Matte finishes, rounded forms, no visible screws. Colour palette: deep aubergine/plum (#3B1F4A), soft lilac (#B9A3E3), warm off-white (#F4F1EC), with one small accent of SOS coral-red (#E5484D) only on the emergency touch points. Wordmark "VEIL" in a thin, wide-spaced sans-serif, debossed (tone on tone, small). No other text or logos. 3:2 or 16:9, high resolution, sharp focus, realistic materials and soft shadows.

## 1. Bracelet - hero shot
Photorealistic product render of the VEIL safety bracelet: a slim, elegant wearable band (like a minimal fitness band crossed with jewellery). Soft-touch silicone strap in deep aubergine. A smooth oval pod on top in matte off-white ceramic-look polymer, about 30 x 22 mm and 9 mm thick. A subtle concave touch pad in the centre with a thin coral-red ring around it (the SOS touch area: hold for 1 second). A tiny pin-hole status LED glowing soft lilac. Small "VEIL" debossed on the side of the pod. Magnetic breakaway clasp in brushed rose-gold metal. Floating at a slight angle on a warm off-white seamless studio background, soft diffused key light, gentle reflection underneath, shallow depth of field.

## 2. Bracelet - exploded view
Technical exploded-view render of the VEIL bracelet pod, parts separated vertically along one axis with thin alignment lines. Layers from top to bottom: off-white top shell with concave touch pad; capacitive touch sensor board; tiny ESP32-C3 microcontroller board; flat coin vibration motor; small curved LiPo battery; USB-C charging board; bottom shell in aubergine with skin-contact window; silicone strap; magnetic clasp halves with a tiny glass reed switch and magnet visible. Clean white background, soft shadows, premium industrial-design presentation style. No text labels.

## 3. Bracelet - on wrist (lifestyle)
Lifestyle photo of a young South Asian woman's wrist wearing the VEIL bracelet (slim aubergine silicone strap, off-white oval pod, coral-red touch ring). Her fingertip is pressing the touch pad. Setting: a dim underground parking basement, softly blurred concrete pillars and cool fluorescent light in the background, warm rim light on the hand. Cinematic, shallow depth of field, calm and empowering mood (not fearful).

## 4. Keychain
Photorealistic render of the VEIL keychain SOS button: a smooth pebble-shaped fob, about 40 x 30 x 12 mm, in matte soft lilac with an off-white top. One large slightly domed coral-red button in the centre. A brushed rose-gold key ring loop at one end. Tiny "VEIL" debossed on the side. Shown with two house keys on the ring, resting on an off-white surface, soft studio light, gentle shadow, 3/4 top view.

## 5. Basement receiver (wall unit)
Photorealistic render of the VEIL basement receiver: a compact wall-mounted unit, about 120 x 80 x 30 mm, rounded rectangle, matte off-white body with an aubergine back plate. A small stubby antenna on top. A thin horizontal light strip on the front glowing soft lilac (standby). A cable exiting from the bottom. Mounted on a concrete pillar in a clean, well-lit underground parking garage, about 2 m high, with a painted parking bay number nearby. Realistic, architectural-photography lighting.

## 6. Guard desk unit
Photorealistic render of the VEIL guard desk console: a small angled desktop unit, about 160 x 100 mm, matte aubergine body with an off-white top face. A 16x2 character LCD window showing "!! SOS ALERT !!" on the top line and "FROM: BRACELET" on the bottom line, in white-on-blue backlit text. Next to it, one large round coral-red push button labelled with an acknowledge icon (no words), a small status LED and a speaker grille for the buzzer. A cable going out the back. Sitting on a security guard's wooden desk with a logbook and a walkie-talkie softly blurred in the background, warm indoor light.

## 7. Phone app screens
Clean UI mockup of a mobile safety app called VEIL, shown on a modern Android phone floating at a slight angle on a lilac gradient background. Three screens side by side: (1) "You are safe" with a large green circle, "Bracelet connected"; (2) full red alert screen "SOS SENT - From your bracelet" with a pulsing red circle, a map location card and buttons "Send location on WhatsApp", "Call guard", "False alarm"; (3) blue screen "HELP IS COMING - The guard has answered". Minimal rounded UI, system font, dark aubergine and off-white theme.

## 8. Full system - hero banner
Wide 16:9 hero render showing the whole VEIL family arranged on a warm off-white studio surface: the bracelet in front-centre, the keychain fob to its left, the wall receiver standing upright behind, the guard desk console to the right with its LCD glowing, and an Android phone showing the red "SOS SENT" app screen. Thin glowing lilac arcs connect the keychain to the bracelet, the bracelet to the receiver, and the receiver to the desk, suggesting a radio link. Premium Apple-style product family shot, soft gradient light, gentle reflections.

## Tips
- If a render adds random text, add: "no text except VEIL and the LCD text".
- If it looks like a smartwatch, add: "no screen on the bracelet, it is a minimal band with a touch pad only".
- For consistency, reuse the CONTEXT block in every new chat.
